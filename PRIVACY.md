# Privacy Policy

Reels Blocker does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The extension uses Chrome's `declarativeNetRequest` API to block short-form video URLs locally in the browser. It does not read page contents, browsing history, cookies, account information, form entries, or messages.

The extension does not include analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, remote code, or third-party services.

User settings, including custom block entries, schedule choices, and preset toggles, are stored only in `chrome.storage.local` on the user's device. The extension does not sync these settings to an account or transmit them to a server.

If the extension is updated in the future to collect any data, this policy must be updated before release.
